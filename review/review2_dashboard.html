Attention as Evidence

Coupling Grad-CAM localisation to vision-language report generation for chest radiographs

Problem

Chest X-ray classifiers report what they see but not where, and report-writing language models make up findings the image does not support. A radiologist cannot trust either without evidence.

Novelty

  • Heatmaps are measured against radiologist boxes and the method is chosen by that score, not by eye.
  • The heatmap is turned into an anatomical zone and handed to MedGemma, so the report is grounded in where the classifier looked.
  • The effect of grounding is quantified on 600 reports, with paired confidence intervals.

Conclusion

Emphysema 0.91, Cardiomegaly 0.89, Edema 0.85, Pneumothorax 0.86 AUROC. Data volume beat every other lever: +0.087 over the sample model.
Weak
Infiltration 0.70, Pneumonia 0.72, Nodule 0.75. Precision is low at 0.29: positives are rare (0.3–24 %) and NIH labels are ~10 % noisy.
Overfit?
no Validation AUROC was still rising at the last epoch (0.827 val, 0.812 test); the sample models plateaued with a small, stable train–val loss gap. Under-trained, not over-fit.
Improve
Train longer and at 320 px (resolution gave +0.025 on the sample); calibrate probabilities; per-finding thresholds tuned to the clinical use.

Conclusion

Strong
Cardiomegaly 9/10, Pneumonia 7/9, Mass 3/4. The layer mattered most: block 4 never passed 23 hits, blocks 3+4 reach 31. More data also improved where the model looks (16 → 31 hits).
Weak
Nodule 0/3, Pneumothorax 2/6, Effusion 3/8. Regions are broad (IoU 0.26): right neighbourhood, little precision.
Overfit?
selection risk 111 configurations ranked on only 53 boxes; the reported hit rate is optimistic until confirmed on unseen boxes.
Improve
Re-score on all 984 NIH boxes; test the 320 px model (sharper features for small findings); report per-finding CIs.

Conclusion

Strong
Grounding works: −1.07 unsupported and −1.0 false findings per report vs scores alone, at nearly the same recall (0.78 vs 0.81). Every report parsed; location follows the zone 93 % of the time.
Weak
Still ~4 false findings per report; normal films are almost never left clean (1 %). Only 5 % of what MedGemma adds beyond the classifier is true.
Overfit?
n/a No training involved. Risk is compliance: the model repeats the zone it is given rather than verifying it.
Improve
Give MedGemma the heatmap image, require evidence per finding, add an abstain rule for normal films; then a blinded human read.
